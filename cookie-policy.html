Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Current use of cookies

This portfolio does not currently set non-essential cookies and does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. No cookie banner is displayed because there are no optional cookies to accept or reject.

2. Essential technical behavior

Your browser and the hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical processes to load pages, protect the service and remember temporary connection information. These processes are required for the website to function and are not used for advertising profiles.

3. External links

When you follow a link to LinkedIn, Instagram, email or another third-party service, that provider may use cookies according to its own policy. This portfolio does not control those external services.

4. Future changes

If analytics, embedded media or other optional technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before those technologies are used.

5. Questions

For questions about this policy, contact higorselvino0@gmail.com.
