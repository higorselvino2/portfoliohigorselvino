Beyond the Sky

A 62-page graphic novel that uses an interplanetary journey to speak about immigration, xenophobia, language barriers and the search for belonging.

Original title: Além do Céu

Science fiction creates the distance needed to look at a present human crisis with empathy.

Year
2025
Role
Comic creation, art direction, illustration, visual identity, editorial design and layout
Context
Graduation project at Anhembi Morumbi University
Team
Higor Selvino, Felipe Moreira Almeida Vieira and Ruan Lucas Ribeiro Silva
Deliverables
Graphic novel, sketchbook, custom box, posters, collectible cards, buttons, bookplate, print and promotional animation
01 · The premise

A foreign child in a divided world

In a retrofuturistic society inspired by the 1940s, Scott meets Zayra, a child who arrived from another planet and cannot speak his language. Their attempt to reach the promised city of Saphira exposes surveillance, forced assimilation and the systems that decide who is allowed to belong.

The story translates immigrant displacement into an interplanetary metaphor. Instead of treating migration as a statistic, it places the reader inside the confusion, isolation and vulnerability of being unable to understand the world around you.

The narrative follows Kishōtenketsu, a four-part structure based on introduction, development, contrast and conclusion rather than a single conventional villain.

Design as a social voice

The project combines research, visual storytelling and editorial craft to build empathy without simplifying the subject.
