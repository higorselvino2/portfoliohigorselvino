Privacy Statement

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. About this website

This is a personal portfolio operated by Higor Selvino. It presents selected design and illustration projects and provides external contact links. The site does not provide user accounts, a newsletter or an internal contact form.

2. Information you provide

If you choose to contact Higor by email or through a linked social platform, the information you send is processed only to answer your message, discuss an opportunity or maintain a professional conversation.

3. Technical information

The hosting provider may automatically process standard server information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, date and time. This information can be used to operate the site, prevent abuse and investigate technical problems.

4. Third-party destinations

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and email services lead to third-party platforms. Their privacy practices and terms apply when you use them.

5. Sharing and sale of data

Personal information is not sold. Information may be disclosed only when required by law, needed to protect the website or handled by essential service providers such as the hosting provider.

6. Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information previously sent directly to Higor, subject to applicable legal obligations. Send the request to higorselvino0@gmail.com.

7. Updates

This statement may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The revision date on this page indicates the latest version.
